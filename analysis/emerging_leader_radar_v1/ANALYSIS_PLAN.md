# Emerging Leader Radar v1 — analysis plan

Collect complete forward sessions, inspect manifests and coverage, freeze this
schema, then preregister candidate rules before confirmation. Keep an untouched
holdout and do not iteratively fit yesterday's leaders.

Compare, for the outside-current-Top100 population:

1. current rank, current percent change, and distance to Top100 alone;
2. rank velocity and acceleration;
3. participation/turnover acceleration;
4. relative-strength and cross-sectional percentile changes;
5. earliness/extension variables;
6. combinations only after individual effects are understood.

Later report precision/recall and PR curves for future entry, median lead time,
false positives/session, cross-symbol/time-of-day/regime robustness, forward
returns, MFE/MAE, transaction costs, slippage, and net expectancy. The ultimate
question is whether a causal post-detection trade could have durable positive
net expectancy, not whether EOD Top100 can be predicted.
